📎 Scan-only sync — attach by Darta No.
When ON, no AI/API is used to read the letter at all — just scan the physical document (or upload a photo) and you'll be asked to type its Darta No. (दर्ता नं.) directly. "Sync to Sheet" then matches each scan to the existing row with that Darta No. and attaches it there. If a Darta No. matches more than one row, you'll get a confirmation popup to pick the right one. If it matches no row, a new row is added automatically with just the Darta No. and the scanned image.
